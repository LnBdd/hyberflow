## Print Orientation

### FDM Print
<p align="center">
  <img src="../Abbildungen/manufacturing/fdm_print-orientation.png" width=600>
</p>

#### Print Settings:
- Layer height: 0.2mm
- Infill: 15%
- Perimeter: 5
- Supports: enabled
- Brim ears: only for large, rectangular parts to prevent warping
